PRIVACY POLICY

Your information and how this site handles it.

Last updated: October 3, 2026

This policy describes information that may be handled when you visit quickservdigital.com or contact QuickServ Digital. The public business name used on this website is QuickServ Digital. The site lists Cambodia as its service area.

Information you choose to provide

The inquiry form opens your device's email application with the details you enter. The website does not submit that message to a form-processing server. Your email provider processes the message when you choose to send it. You may also contact us directly by email or Telegram; those services handle your communications under their own privacy terms.

Website interaction data

The site may queue basic interaction events in the browser, such as the page path, event type, timestamp, and the label or destination of a link. The inquiry event records the selected service, not your name, email address, or message. If an analytics or tag-management provider is connected, it may receive event data under its own terms. Do not include sensitive information in a link or message unless needed for your inquiry.

Hosting and third-party services

When a page is requested, website hosting and security providers may process technical request information to deliver and protect the site. Links to email, Telegram, social networks, advertising platforms, and other services take you to providers with their own policies.

Use and retention

Information sent to us may be used to reply to your inquiry, prepare a proposal, and provide agreed services. Messages are retained in the communication systems used to receive them for as long as reasonably needed for those purposes and applicable recordkeeping obligations.

Your choices

You can choose not to submit an inquiry or can contact us through another available channel. For a privacy question or request about information you sent directly to QuickServ Digital, email contact@quickservdigital.com. Applicable privacy rights depend on the laws that apply to you.

Policy updates

We may revise this policy when the site or its data practices change. The date at the top identifies the latest published version.
